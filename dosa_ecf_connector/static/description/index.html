Dosa e-CF Connector

Electronic invoicing (e-CF) for the Dominican Republic's tax authority (DGII), via Dosa Invoice Cloud. Issue, record and verify your electronic tax receipts without leaving Odoo.

Automatic or manual submission

Issue E31, E32, E34 (credit notes) and E46 (export) receipts directly from your Odoo invoices. You choose: automatic submission on validation, or a button on the invoice itself — no extra screens.

Automatic vendor bill import

Periodically syncs the electronic receipts issued by your suppliers and records them as vendor bills in Odoo, with taxes detected automatically per line.

DGII verification QR code

Adds the official DGII verification QR code to Odoo's native invoice report — works alongside your existing layout/logo, without replacing it.

Export & construction-sector support

Dedicated handling for export receipts (E46, with foreign-currency amounts) and for the special ITBIS regime for the construction sector (Norma 07-07).

Submission monitor

A single list of every submitted receipt: DGII status (Accepted, Accepted with observations, Conditionally accepted, Rejected), track ID, DGII messages and communication failures — without opening each invoice individually.

eNCF number ranges

Configure the authorized from/to range and the expiration date of each eNCF sequence per receipt type from a single screen, with automatic validation before submission.

External service & data disclosure

This app requires an account with Dosa Invoice Cloud (dosasystem.com) and sends it the data needed to issue an e-CF: your company's invoice, customer and product data (names, tax IDs/RNC, addresses, line items and amounts) for outgoing invoices, and reads back the corresponding vendor invoice data for receipts issued to you. No data is sent until you configure your own API key and either validate an invoice or enable automatic submission.

Requirements

  • Odoo 19 Community or Enterprise, with the Accounting/Invoicing app installed.
  • An active Dosa Invoice Cloud account (dosasystem.com) with an API key.
